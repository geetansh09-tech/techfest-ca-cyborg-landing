// 01 — Mission Brief

You're Not a Volunteer. You're a Node in the Network.

Every year, Techfest recruits a distributed network of student ambassadors to carry its signal into every corner of the country — and every ambassador upgrades their own skill-set in the process.

As a Campus Ambassador, you become Techfest's local node — the person who translates a global tech festival into real action on your campus: promoting competitions, driving registrations, and building a community of builders around you.

It's part marketing, part leadership training, part networking accelerator. Past CAs have gone on to lead startups, land internships through Techfest's partner network, and build the kind of self-driven initiative that no lecture can teach.

// 05 — FAQ Log

Frequently Decoded Questions

Who can apply to be a Campus Ambassador? +

Any currently enrolled college or university student, from any year or discipline. No prior experience is required — just willingness to lead and represent your campus.

Is there any cost to join the CA Program? +

No. The program is completely free to join — your only investment is time and effort toward outreach on your campus.

How is my performance tracked? +

Through a unique referral code and your CA dashboard, which logs registrations, event participation, and outreach activities in real time.

Can I be a CA for multiple years? +

Yes — many ambassadors return year after year, climbing tiers and taking on larger leadership roles like Campus Lead.
